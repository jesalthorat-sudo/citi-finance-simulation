# Citi Finance Job Simulation: Four Analyst Tasks

I completed the Citi Finance virtual job simulation on Forage. This is **not work experience at Citi**. It is a set of four finance analyst tasks, and this repo holds my finished work for each one.

## What's inside

| Task | Question | File | What I found |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. KPI review | Why did revenue, expenses, cost of credit and net income move in 2022? | `Citi_KPI_Review_2022.pptx` | Revenue rose 6% and expenses fell 4%, but a $2.3bn swing in cost of credit cut net income 21% to $2,513m. |
| 2. Capital check | Are the business units' capital ratios correct? | `RWA_Verification.xlsx` and `Task2_Email_to_Manager.md` | All four units used the wrong risk weight for card loans (25% instead of 100%). Units C and D fall below the 10.5% minimum. |
| 3. Omicron impact | Will COVID cases hurt credit card sales? | `Omicron_Impact_Credit_Card_Sales.pptx` | Cases and spending are only weakly linked. The risk to card sales is low and mostly in social and work-related spending. |
| 4. Limits monitoring | Were any deposit or loan limits breached? | `Treasury_Triggers_Report.pptx` | Entity 1 hit a Management Action Trigger from 15 Feb (surplus of $(19.1)m on 28 Feb). Entity 2 is at its warning limit. Entity 3 is fine. |

## Skills shown
- Explaining financial results to senior managers
- Basel III capital adequacy and risk-weighted assets
- Scenario analysis and charts
- Liquidity limit monitoring
- Excel and PowerPoint

## Notes
- The data came from the job simulation and is illustrative.
- Task 3 uses spending data that ends in Dec 2021, so the Omicron view is an estimate from past waves, not a measured result.
